## 💡 What Is a "Fund"? (In Plain English)
Think of investing like buying fruit:
* **Buying single stocks** is like trying to guess which single apple on a tree won't go bad. If that one company runs into trouble, your savings take a hit.
* **Buying a fund** is like buying a pre-packaged basket of hundreds of the world's best companies (Apple, Microsoft, AstraZeneca, Shell) and government bonds. Even if one company has a bad year, the others balance it out.

**The Dutta UK Funds Selection Advisor** is your free, jargon-free co-pilot that helps you choose the best baskets for your life goals and shows you how long your money will last after you retire.

## 🚀 3 Quick Steps to Plan Your Wealth (Takes Under 60 Seconds)

### Step 1: Tell Us About Your Life Stage
On the left sidebar, enter:
1. **Your Current Age** (e.g. 35) and **Target Retirement Age** (e.g. 65).
2. **Starting Lump Sum**: Any savings you have ready today (or £0 if starting fresh).
3. **Monthly Savings**: What you can comfortably tuck away each month (e.g. £300/month).

### Step 2: Choose Your Comfort Level (Risk Appetite)
Pick one of 3 simple risk profiles:
* 🟢 **Low Risk (Capital Preservation)**: Best if you are close to retirement or lose sleep over market swings. Focuses on safe government bonds and money market cash to beat inflation gently.
* 🔵 **Medium Risk (Balanced Core)**: The classic 60/40 mix. Half in growing global companies, half in safe bonds. Steady growth with a shock absorber.
* 🟣 **High Risk (Maximum Growth)**: Best if you have 10–25+ years ahead of you. 100% invested in top global companies and tech leaders for maximum compounding.

### Step 3: Compare "Active" vs. "Passive"
* 🏆 **Active Funds (Expert Hand-Picked)**: Veteran fund managers (like Terry Smith or James Thomson) analyze companies daily to pick only the winners. They charge a small fee (~0.7%–0.9%) to beat the market.
* ⚡ **Passive Trackers (Low-Cost Robots)**: Automatic computer trackers (like Vanguard or iShares) that simply copy the entire world stock market for tiny fees (~0.06%–0.2%). Zero manager guesswork.
