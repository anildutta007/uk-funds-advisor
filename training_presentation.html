Module 3 • Defeating the Inflation Illusion

"Nominal Money" vs. "Today's World" Value

Why the green badge is your most important benchmark on the entire platform:

Projected Future Number (Nominal)
£842,500

The total cash figure on your bank statement in 30 years.

"Today's World" Purchasing Power (@ 3% Inflation)
£347,200

What that £842,500 will actually buy in groceries, energy bills, dining out, and housing in today's currency!

The Discounting Math Under the Hood

The app applies the standard Bank of England long-term inflation target ($r = 3\%$):

PV = FV / (1 + 0.03)^Years

Why it protects you: If you only plan around the big future number, you will unintentionally under-save and face a shock at retirement. The green badge keeps your standard of living realistic.

Rule of Thumb: Always budget your retirement lifestyle using "Today's World" values.
Module 4 • Core Portfolios (Tabs 1 & 2)

Active Management vs. Passive Indexing

Choose your investment philosophy, inspect fee drag, or swap funds safely:

Tab 1: Active Fund Strategy Human Managers

Star fund managers (e.g. Terry Smith, Royal London Sustainable Leaders) hand-picking quality companies to beat the index.

  • Pros: Potential to outperform market drops; high FE Crown ratings.
  • Fee Drag: Typically 0.75% – 0.85% OCF annual fee.
Tab 2: Passive Index Strategy Automated Trackers

Rule-based funds (e.g. Vanguard FTSE Global All Cap, S&P 500) mechanically mirroring the whole market.

  • Pros: Ultra-low fee drag (0.07% – 0.22% OCF); guarantees market returns.
  • Fee Drag: Minimal drag, allowing compound growth to maximize.
The "Swap Fund" Safety Guardrail: Clicking "Swap Fund" only shows sister funds from Trustnet with the exact same risk level so you can't accidentally destabilize your portfolio.
Module 5 • HMRC Tax Architecture

Understanding the 3 HMRC Account Structures

How you take money out determines how much HMRC keeps:

1 Most Popular

UK SIPP (25% UFPLS)

Uncrystallised Funds Pension Lump Sum. Every monthly withdrawal is automatically split:

✓ 25% Tax-Free Cash
✓ 75% Taxable Income

Use if you haven't taken your tax-free cash upfront.

2 Post-Lump Sum

100% Taxable Drawdown

For retirees who already cashed out their full 25% tax-free lump sum upfront on day 1 (e.g. paying off a mortgage).

100% of withdrawals are assessed against standard UK income tax bands.

Utilizes Personal Allowance (£12,570).

3 Zero Tax Ever

Stocks & Shares ISA

Funded with post-tax savings (up to £20,000/year).

£0.00 tax to HMRC forever. Withdrawals never impact your tax bracket!

Total drawdown freedom.
