Advisory Methodology & UK Regulatory Disclosures

1. Performance & Growth Calculations: The expected future values displayed are computed using compound growth formulas based on the average annualised compound growth rate (CAGR) of each fund over the last 15 years (or since fund inception if operational for fewer years). Historical returns are calculated net of ongoing charges (OCF) in Sterling (£ GBP). Past performance is not a reliable indicator or guarantee of future returns.

2. Trustnet (FE fundinfo) Data & Platform Accessibility: Fund metrics including Total Fund Size (AUM), Inception Dates, Lead Manager Tenure, Citicodes, FE Crown Ratings (1 to 5 Crowns), and FE fundinfo Risk Scores (relative to FTSE 100 benchmark = 100) are compiled from authentic fund factsheets on Trustnet.com and official Investment Association (IA) sector benchmarks. These funds are liquid, regulated, and widely accessible via major UK investment platforms including Hargreaves Lansdown, AJ Bell, Interactive Investor, and Fidelity.

3. Non-Advice Notice: This software is an analytical planning tool designed strictly for financial education and self-directed investor research. It does not consider individual tax status, debt obligations, or personal financial hardship. For bespoke advice, consult an Independent Financial Adviser (IFA) authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA).
